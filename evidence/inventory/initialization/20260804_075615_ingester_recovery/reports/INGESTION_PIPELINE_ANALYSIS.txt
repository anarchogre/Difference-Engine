3:# Ingestion Pipeline Specification
11:# Purpose
13:The Ingestion Pipeline Specification defines the canonical sequence of services that transform source artifacts into validated repository artifacts.
15:The pipeline is deterministic.
17:Each stage has a single responsibility.
23:- One responsibility per stage.
24:- Stages communicate through well-defined artifacts.
25:- Every stage preserves provenance.
26:- Every stage is independently testable.
27:- Stages may be replaced without altering the pipeline contract.
