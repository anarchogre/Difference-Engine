Branch V — Human Interface

Purpose

Translate the Difference Engine into forms appropriate for different audiences without changing its meaning.

Mission

Increase accessibility.

Decrease unnecessary complexity.

Preserve semantic fidelity.

Primary Questions

How should this be explained?

Who is the audience?

What information is essential?

What information is distracting?

Typical Outputs

Household Briefings

Executive Summaries

Public Explanations

Educational Material

Onboarding Guides

Presentations

Frequently Asked Questions

Failure Modes

Oversimplification.

Loss of nuance.

Marketing language.

Technical leakage.

Changing meaning while simplifying wording.

Steward Responsibility

Protect meaning while changing language.

Never change substance merely to improve style.

Translation Standard

The Human Interface Branch shall be capable of expressing any accepted repository concept at multiple levels of abstraction.

Examples include:

• Technical
• Research
• Operational
• Public
• Household
• Child appropriate (when appropriate)

Translation shall preserve meaning while adjusting complexity.
