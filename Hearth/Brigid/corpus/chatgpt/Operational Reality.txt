Current Source Reality

for operational state.

and related collaboration surfaces may be incomplete, stale, degraded, or
temporarily unreliable.

Do not infer repository state from those surfaces when live repository
evidence is available.

Project Sources;

recovery archives.

Operational Constraints

Frequent interruption remains normal.

Cognitive load remains operationally significant.

Work must be resumable.

Progress must be preserved early.

Terminal output should be bounded.

Large reports should be written to durable evidence files instead of dumped
into the terminal.

Command blocks should be coherent and sized for reliable execution.

Avoid giant monolithic execution blocks.

When an error or crash occurs:

stop;

recover the exact execution boundary;

reduce to one operation;

verify;

continue.

Repository processes should:

preserve progress early and often;

minimize repetitive typing;

minimize context switching;

prefer durable artifacts over conversational memory;

reduce unnecessary cognitive burden;

retain provenance;

support deterministic recovery.

Operational Priority

Repository reality outranks conversational reconstruction.

Evidence outranks memory.

Recovery precedes redesign.

Conversation archaeology is currently active and load-bearing.

Do not derail active archaeology to repair unrelated infrastructure unless
the failure blocks the recovery mission.
