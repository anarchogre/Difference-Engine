# Glossary

## ADE

A Difference Engine, the umbrella project and canonical repository.

## Canonical

The authoritative version of a document or artifact.

## Preservation Point (PP)

A stable historical checkpoint representing the repository at a
meaningful milestone.

## Repository Constitution (RC)

Foundational governance documents that define repository structure and
rules.

## Semantic Continuity

Maintaining meaning across revisions even when wording or organization
changes.
