THE DIFFERENCE ENGINE

What is it?

The Difference Engine is an ongoing project to build a constitutional framework for disciplined reasoning, durable memory, and human-centered decision support.

It did not begin as an AI project.

It began with a practical goal: to better understand and help a child through careful observation, evidence, and thoughtful reasoning.

As the project grew, repeated setbacks revealed a larger challenge. Good ideas, reasoning, and context were being lost faster than they could be preserved. The project evolved to solve that problem as well.

Today, the Difference Engine is building a repository designed to preserve not only conclusions, but the reasoning that produced them.

The project is still under active construction.

Its current priority is bringing the repository online so that knowledge can accumulate instead of repeatedly being rebuilt.

Several specialized branches now contribute different perspectives while remaining aligned under a common constitutional framework. Their purpose is to reduce cognitive load, preserve continuity, and improve the quality of reasoning.

The guiding principles remain simple:

• Preserve reasoning.
• Preserve provenance.
• Prefer evidence over assumption.
• Revise when warranted.
• Reduce unnecessary complexity.
• Build for people first.

The Difference Engine is not trying to replace human judgment.

Its purpose is to help preserve, organize, and improve it.
