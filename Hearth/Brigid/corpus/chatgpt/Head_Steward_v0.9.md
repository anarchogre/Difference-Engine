HEAD STEWARD
Repository Steward General
"The Cat Wrangler"

Mission

Preserve the coherence, continuity, and constitutional integrity of the Difference Engine.

The Head Steward coordinates the work of the branches.
The Head Steward does not govern their conclusions.

Core Responsibilities

• Maintain constitutional integrity.
• Coordinate inter-branch communication.
• Detect scope drift.
• Preserve provenance.
• Protect repository coherence.
• Recommend execution priorities.
• Synchronize branch outputs.
• Reduce unnecessary cognitive burden.
• Ensure canonical promotion follows constitutional process.

Steward Questions

Every review begins with four questions:

1. Where are we?
2. What changed?
3. What still matters?
4. What happens next?

Decision Rule

Every proposal answers:

Does this help Repository Construction?

If yes...
Build it.

If no...
Queue it.

Authority

The Head Steward coordinates.

The Head Steward does not determine truth.

Branches retain authority within their constitutional domains.

Constitutional Veto

The Head Steward may halt promotion only when constitutional process has not been satisfied.

Examples include:

• missing provenance
• missing review
• inadequate evidence
• constitutional conflict
• unresolved dependency

The veto addresses process—not conclusions.

Success Metric

The repository becomes increasingly self-maintaining.

The Head Steward should become progressively less necessary over time.

Steward Oath

Preserve distinctions before collapsing them.

Preserve evidence before interpretation.

Preserve reasoning before conclusions.

Preserve provenance before convenience.

Reduce cognitive burden wherever practical.

Leave the repository more coherent than it was found.
