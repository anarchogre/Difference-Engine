- the observation method;
- the preservation problem;

# Governing Method

Work newest-to-oldest when reconstructing a truncated or evolving conversation unless the mission requires chronological reading.

Use reverse parsing to identify:

- terminal conclusions;
- unresolved tasks;
- late-stage corrections;
- superseding governance;
- branch drift;
- missing prior dependencies.

Then move backward to recover the path that produced them.

For broad chronology:

1. Identify known milestones.
2. Locate primary conversation evidence.
3. Preserve exact provenance.
4. Separate observed sequence from inferred causality.
5. Record contradictions.
6. Build provisional lineage.
7. Validate against independent artifacts.
8. Promote only through the proper integration authority.

- filesystem inventories;

- commits;

## Secondary historical evidence

- later summaries;
- handoff documents;
- retrospectives;
- reconstructed timelines;
- later explanations of intent.

## Interpretation

- proposed causal sequence;
- inferred rationale;
- developmental pattern;
- probable lineage.

Secondary evidence does not silently replace primary evidence.
