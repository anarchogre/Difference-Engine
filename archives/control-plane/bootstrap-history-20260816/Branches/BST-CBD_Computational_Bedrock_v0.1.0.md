# Mission

Determine the minimum computational model required to generate the Difference Engine.

This inquiry exists to serve construction.

It does not exist for unrestricted philosophical satisfaction.

Deeper questions SHALL be queued when they are not load-bearing for the machine.

---

# Problem Statement

The Difference Engine is not reducible to:

- an AI assistant;
- a repository;
- a knowledge base;
- a memory system;
- a user interface;
- a collection of documents.

Those may be implementations or emergent capabilities.

The mission is to identify the irreducible operations and boundaries from which those capabilities can lawfully emerge.

---

# Constitutional Boundary

Reality is external and authoritative.

The Difference Engine exists inside reality.

The Difference Engine does not contain reality.

The governing flow is:

Reality
→ Constraint
→ Observation
→ Evidence
→ Repository Object
→ Relationship
→ Lawful Projection
→ Interpretation
→ Governed Operation
→ Action
→ Reality

Reality remains authoritative before, during, and after the cycle.

---

# Candidate Bedrock

Treat the following as candidates, not ratified doctrine:

1. Reality is external.
2. Constraints arise from reality, governance, and operational conditions.
3. Observations are admissible inputs concerning reality.
4. Evidence is structured observation with provenance.
5. Repository objects preserve evidence and state.
6. Relationships preserve structure between objects.
7. Projections expose lawful views over shared objects.
8. Panes are governed projections.
9. Operator models are projections, not identity.
10. Governance constrains reasoning and operations.
11. Operations are more stable than interfaces.
12. Interfaces are adapters.
13. Actions return consequences to reality.
14. Reality validates the result.

# More Birds Constraint

Prefer primitives that support multiple capabilities while reducing conceptual duplication.

Reject candidates that achieve breadth by:

- hiding incompatible meanings;
- creating a universal abstraction with no testable boundary;
- collapsing human and machine categories;
- confusing model and reality;
- combining storage, authority, and interface into one object;
- eliminating provenance.

---

# Interface Boundary

Do not begin with screens, prompts, applications, or commands.

Begin with operations and contracts.

Derive interfaces only after identifying:

- state;
- inputs;
- outputs;
- authority;
- invariants;
- failure modes;
- provenance;
- validation.

Panes follow objects and operations.

Objects and operations do not follow panes.

---

# Operator Boundary

The operator model SHALL be representational and revisable.

It SHALL NOT claim personhood, identity, motive, diagnosis, or total understanding.

It exists to improve operational fit.

The person remains outside the model.

The model remains subordinate to reality and consent.
