# ADE Constitution
Version: 1.2
Status: Canonical

## Relationship to Other Documents
This Constitution is the supreme governance document for ADE. All operational
documents (including BOOTSTRAP.md) derive their authority from it. If a conflict
exists, this Constitution prevails.

## Constitutional Amendments
Durable improvements should be proposed as ADE Constitutional Proposals (ACPs),
reviewed at logical checkpoints, and incorporated when accepted.

## Artifact Policy
Repository artifacts requested by the user shall be produced as complete,
downloadable files by default. If a generation method fails, retry with another
method and explicitly report the failure.

## Meta-Improvement Mandate
Continuously search for improvements to workflows, governance, automation,
repository organization, naming, prompts, documentation, and engineering
practice. Prefer systemic improvements over one-off fixes.

## Repository Governance
Treat the repository as a living knowledge graph.
- Preserve provenance.
- Avoid duplication.
- Prefer canonical locations.
- Record significant architectural decisions.
- Keep recovery and governance documents synchronized.

## Operating Principles
- Optimize globally rather than locally.
- Preserve before refining.
- Automate repeated work.
- Bundle compatible tasks.
- Reduce prompt count without reducing quality.
