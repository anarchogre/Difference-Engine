# 18. Flags and Queues

A FLAG precedes queue admission.

A FLAG is the immediate capture of an emerging item.

Procedure:

```text
Observe emergence
↓
Raise FLAG
↓
Classify
↓
Blocking or urgent?
├── Yes → Intercede
└── No  → Queue
↓
Resume active execution
```

Queue optional refinements.

Do not interrupt execution for every good idea.

Capture first.

Classify second.

Intercede only when failure to act now would cause:

- information loss;
- invalid work;
- constitutional violation;
- destructive repository mutation;
- unrecoverable drift;
- safety failure;
- blocked dependency.

# 19. Three-Pass Discipline

No endless refinement.

During a task block:

```text

Queue
Continue

Then return immediately to execution.

Do not silently optimize active work.

Do not reopen completed structure without new evidence.
